# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (RPD)

## Qur’an & Sunnah Based Ruqyah Web Platform

**Project Type:** Web-based Islamic Ruqyah & Educational Platform  
**Primary Market:** Bangladesh  
**Primary Language:** Bangla  
**Secondary Language:** English — Future Phase  
**Platform:** Responsive Web Application  
**Target Devices:** Desktop, Tablet & Mobile Browser  
**Admin:** Web-based Admin Dashboard  
**Future Expansion:** PWA / Mobile App / AI Assistant / Practitioner Network

---

# 1. Project Overview

The objective of this project is to build a professional, trustworthy and user-friendly web platform dedicated to **Ruqyah Shar’iyyah, Qur’an, authentic Sunnah, Dua, Adhkar and Islamic guidance**.

The platform will not be positioned merely as a Ruqyah treatment/clinic website.

Instead, it will function as a complete digital ecosystem where users can:

- Learn what Ruqyah Shar’iyyah is
- Understand what Islam says about common spiritual concerns
- Access Qur’anic verses and authentic Duas
- Listen to Ruqyah audio
- Learn Self-Ruqyah step-by-step
- Explore educational articles
- Find answers to common questions
- Perform a guided self-assessment
- Book Ruqyah-related services where applicable
- Find verified practitioners in the future
- Save useful resources
- Build a personal Ruqyah/Adhkar routine
- Understand fraudulent and non-Shariah practices
- Know when professional medical/mental-health assistance may also be appropriate

The core philosophy will be:

> **Qur’an & Authentic Sunnah • Tawheed • Shariah Compliance • Education • Safety • Privacy • Trust**

## Vision Statement

To create one of Bangladesh’s most trusted and comprehensive web-based resources for **Qur’an and authentic Sunnah-based Ruqyah education and guidance**, making reliable Islamic resources accessible to ordinary users in a simple, organized and modern digital experience.

# 4. Core Product Principles

Every major feature must follow these principles.

### 4.1 Qur’an & Authentic Sunnah

Content should be based on:

- Qur’an
- Authentic Hadith
- Established Islamic scholarship
- Reliable scholarly references

### 4.2 No Superstition

The platform must actively distinguish Shariah-based Ruqyah from:

- Shirk
- Talismans/Taweez where inappropriate
- Unknown incantations
- Magic practices
- Fortune telling
- Astrology
- Jinn-calling practices
- Unverified spiritual rituals

### 4.3 No Guaranteed Cure Claims

The platform must never promise:

> “100% guaranteed cure”

or imply that every physical/psychological symptom is caused by Jinn, Sihr or Evil Eye.

### 4.4 Medical Safety

Users should be clearly informed that Ruqyah does not automatically replace:

- Medical diagnosis
- Emergency medical care
- Psychiatric care
- Psychological counselling
- Other qualified healthcare services

### 4.5 Privacy

Ruqyah-related user information can be highly sensitive.

Therefore:

- Minimal data collection
- Secure storage
- Privacy policy
- Controlled admin access
- No unnecessary public exposure
- No public display of personal case information

must be implemented.

**Ruqyah Knowledge Assistant**

Not:

“AI Raqi”

Not:

“AI Healer”

- What is Ruqyah?
- How do I start Self-Ruqyah?
- Which resources are relevant?
- Where is this Dua sourced from?
- What does this article mean?
- Which section should I read?

### AI must not:

- Diagnose Jinn
- Diagnose Sihr
- Diagnose Evil Eye
- Guarantee cure
- Replace doctors
- Invent Islamic rulings

Then gradually evolve into:

**Personal Dashboard + Practitioner Network + AI Knowledge Assistant + PWA/Mobile Ecosystem.**
